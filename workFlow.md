## 🧭 Perspective Workflows

<details open>
<summary><b>🌱 1. Farmer / Producer Lifecycle</b></summary>
<br>

* `OnboardingPage.jsx` — Geo-tag farm location (1–5 km discovery radius) & verify producer authenticity.
* `ProducePage.jsx` — Post lots with live harvest timestamps *(e.g., "Harvested at 06:20 AM · 38 kg")* & cultivation type.
* `OrdersPage.jsx` — Manage live state: `Placed` ➔ `Confirmed` ➔ `Preparing` ➔ `Request Pickup`.
* `WalletPage.jsx` — Instant earnings settlement with automated 2% take-rate transparency.

</details>

<details open>
<summary><b>🛒 2. Consumer Experience</b></summary>
<br>

* `HomePage.jsx` / `FarmersPage.jsx` — Discover verified neighborhood growers with 1–3 hr delivery SLA.
* `FarmerDetailPage.jsx` / `ProductDetailPage.jsx` — Inspect farm origin, soil practices, and morning harvest logs.
* `CartPage.jsx` / `CheckoutPage.jsx` — Clean transparent checkout with UPI, Card, and POD options.
* `TrackingPage.jsx` — 4-stage live tracker: `Confirmed` ➔ `Packing` ➔ `In-Transit` ➔ `Delivered`.

</details>

<details open>
<summary><b>🚴 3. Fleet & Dispatch Operation</b></summary>
<br>

* `RiderDashboard.jsx` — Accept batch pings with distance, estimated time, and fixed payout breakdown.
* `ActiveDeliveryPage.jsx` — Turn-by-turn routing: Farm Hub Pickup ➔ Order Verification ➔ Doorstep Handover.
* **Shift Console** — Real-time performance metrics tracking daily trips, ETA accuracy, and rating index.

</details>
